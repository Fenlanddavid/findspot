# LAND-ACCESS decision record

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: Phase A implemented; Phase B demand-gated and not started

## Product decision

FindSpot will help a user obtain and retain permission through knowledge and
existing report/agreement tooling. It will not become a landowner marketplace,
contact database, permission exchange or landowner-rating service.

Phase A is static guidance with no engine and no new durable state. It is
independent of any registered-title dataset and must remain useful if Phase B
never ships.

## Phase A boundaries

- The guide is a reference surface, not a wizard or tracked workflow.
- A refusal is final. The copy does not encourage unannounced private-home
  visits, pressure, repeated approaches or changing contact channel after a
  refusal.
- Owner and occupier are treated separately where land is tenanted or managed.
- HM Land Registry is a user-run link-out. FindSpot does not obtain or retain
  proprietor data.
- The agreement is an editable starting template, not legal advice.
- Existing permission reports are framed as reports to promise and provide to
  the landowner. No report engine is added.
- Guidance is expressly scoped to England and Wales where it refers to HM Land
  Registry, PAS and the Treasure Act process.

## Phase B hold

The registered-boundary layer is not part of Phase A or the V5 release. Before
licence Stage 0 begins, a plain-language demand probe must establish that users
understand both that a registered proprietor may not be the occupier and that a
boundary says nothing about permission to detect.

If the demand gate clears, Phase B still requires written rulings for HM Land
Registry and Ordnance Survey geometry terms, hosting, derivation, app-store
distribution and offline caching before any production code or data download.

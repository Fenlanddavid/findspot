FindSpot Companion

Privacy policy · effective 1 August 2026

FindSpot Companion records GPS trails on your Android device. It is local-first and does not send your location or recordings to FindSpot or to any analytics service.

Information the app uses

The app uses precise and approximate location while you choose to record a trail, including while the screen is locked. Location observations, times, accuracy information and recording state are saved locally on your device so that recording can survive screen locking, interruption and reboot.

Network access and tracking

The Companion app has no internet permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, account system or third-party tracking SDK. The developer does not receive or collect data from the Companion app.

Sharing and retention

A recording leaves the Companion app only when you explicitly use Android's share or export controls. You choose the destination, which can include FindSpot or another app installed on your device. Unexported recordings remain on the device until you delete them. Exported, stopped recordings may be removed automatically after 30 days.

Your control

You can stop recording at any time, delete local recordings, revoke location or notification permission in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

Children

FindSpot Companion is a field-recording utility intended for adults. It is not directed to children.

Questions

For privacy or support questions, use the FindSpot support issue tracker.
